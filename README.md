RENATE Open Diagnostics, or in short RENATE, is a physics-based modelling suite for the beam emission spectroscopy diagnostic of magnetically confined high temperature plasmas. 

RENATE is being developed in Python, strictly following the policies detailed in the 
programming_giudelines.pdf.

RENATE-OD is being developed under the GPL. However, to avoid misuse, we ask that you contact the responsible developer prior to any scientific applications. We are just as open to collaboration as our code is open for development!
